CURATED RECOMMENDATIONS

Where I Would Send My Friends

Honest recommendations based on more than a decade of exploring every corner of Nicaragua.

Best beaches

Remanso for gentle waves and sunset beers, Marsella for peaceful solitude, Yankee for uncrowded surf, Madera for lively vibes, and Marajual for breathtaking evening sunsets.

Things to do

World-class surfing, adrenaline volcano boarding at Cerro Negro, cloud forest volcano hiking, deep-sea fishing, and vibrant snorkeling or diving off pristine shores.

Hidden gems

The rugged coastlines of Chinandega, the festive cowboy culture of Boaco, the refreshing mountain air and waterfalls of Matagalpa, and the artisanal traditions of Camoapa.

THE ZIONNICA STORY

Discover. Explore. Connect. Put Down Roots.

Welcome to ZIONNICA—a place where travel, nature, community, and the pursuit of a meaningful life come together.

My love for travel began in college, when I had the opportunity to study abroad in Switzerland. What started as one experience quickly turned into a passion for seeing the world. During my time in Europe, I traveled throughout much of the EU, and when I returned home after college, I made myself a promise: as long as I was able, I would visit one or two new countries every year.

For more than a decade, I kept that promise—and often exceeded it. Today, I've had the opportunity to visit more than 60 countries. I've experienced incredible places, met amazing people, eaten unforgettable food, and learned that sometimes the most meaningful parts of travel aren't the places you check off a list, but the places that keep calling you back.

And for me, one of those places has been Nicaragua. For more than ten years, I've traveled throughout Nicaragua and Central America. I've returned to places I've loved, explored places I'd never seen, and spent time getting to know the people and communities that make these countries special, revisiting consistently every 3 to 6 months.

"Over time, I found myself facing a dilemma that many travelers eventually encounter: Do I keep chasing new destinations, or do I return to the places that already feel like home?"

Eventually, I decided to put more than just my time into that relationship—I purchased property near the coast. Today, Nicaragua isn't simply somewhere I enjoy visiting; it's a place where I'm building toward a future.

I jokingly created my own word for what I'm working toward: The Sandbird—part snowbird, part beach bum, part traveler. The idea is simple: live between two places I love. Spend part of my time in Florida, part of my time in Nicaragua, and build a life that allows me to enjoy the best of both worlds.

Along the way, I discovered that many people share this exact dream: travelers, second-home seekers, retirees, and people looking to escape the traditional nine-to-five routine. ZIONNICA was created to share what I've learned—from finding vetted attorneys and realtors to hidden beaches, local food, and self-guided itineraries.

"And who knows? Maybe one day I'll see you somewhere in Nicaragua, sitting on a beach, enjoying a cold Toña, and wondering why we didn't do this sooner."
