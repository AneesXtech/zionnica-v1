AUTHENTIC EXPERIENCES

Unforgettable Adventures in Nicaragua.

From volcano boarding down black ash slopes to uncrowded Pacific breaks and cloud forest canopy treks, immerse yourself in Nicaragua's most iconic adventures.

VOLCANOES Cerro Negro, León

Volcano Boarding at Cerro Negro

Hike the black crater rim and carve down Central America's youngest active volcano on a custom wooden board at speeds up to 60 km/h.

Pace: High Thrill Season: Year-Round
SURF & COASTAL Popoyo & SJDS

World-Class Pacific Wave Riding

Experience 300+ days of steady offshore winds creating legendary point breaks and beginner-friendly beach rollers at Remanso and Maderas.

Level: All Levels Peak: May – Nov
NATURE & HIKING Isla de Ometepe

Ometepe Cloud Forest Trekking

Trek ancient pre-Columbian petroglyphs and mist-shrouded crater waterfalls on Volcán Maderas, surrounded by howler monkeys and orchids.

Guide: Recommended Trail: Crater Lagoon
DIVING & ISLANDS Big & Little Corn

Caribbean Coral Reef Diving

Immerse in turquoise waters with nurse sharks, eagle rays, and untouched coral gardens alongside relaxed Creole culture and fresh lobster.

Visibility: 30m+ Style: Island Escape

THE STORY BEHIND ZIONNICA

A Journey That Led Me to Nicaragua.

After exploring more than 60 countries, I discovered something truly special in Nicaragua. For over a decade, I've returned to experience its beauty, culture and people. What started as a travel adventure gradually became a deeper connection. A connection that continues to bring me back.

Today, Nicaragua is more than just a destination for me. It's a place where I've built memories and found new possibilities. Through ZIONNICA, I share my personal journey and discoveries. And invite you to experience Nicaragua in your own way.

Discover the possibilities of owning property in Nicaragua.

From choosing a location to due diligence and legal guidance.

“Ask, trust, but verify.”The founder's cardinal rule
1. Find the right location

Match the place to your lifestyle: Pacific cliffs, surf parcels or colonial Granada.

2. Buy property and title

Understand fee-simple title (Escritura Pública), contracts and escrow.

3. Get legal guidance

Check 30 years of title history, surveys and tax clearance, with a lawyer you trust.

4. Build or manage a home

Visit finished projects before choosing a builder, and find a reliable rental manager.

This reflects personal ownership experience. Always hire independent legal counsel and do your own due diligence.

THE ZIONNICA STORY

Discover. Explore. Connect. Put Down Roots.

Welcome to ZIONNICA—a place where travel, nature, community, and the pursuit of a meaningful life come together.

My love for travel began in college, when I had the opportunity to study abroad in Switzerland. What started as one experience quickly turned into a passion for seeing the world. During my time in Europe, I traveled throughout much of the EU, and when I returned home after college, I made myself a promise: as long as I was able, I would visit one or two new countries every year.

For more than a decade, I kept that promise—and often exceeded it. Today, I've had the opportunity to visit more than 60 countries. I've experienced incredible places, met amazing people, eaten unforgettable food, and learned that sometimes the most meaningful parts of travel aren't the places you check off a list, but the places that keep calling you back.

And for me, one of those places has been Nicaragua. For more than ten years, I've traveled throughout Nicaragua and Central America. I've returned to places I've loved, explored places I'd never seen, and spent time getting to know the people and communities that make these countries special, revisiting consistently every 3 to 6 months.

"Over time, I found myself facing a dilemma that many travelers eventually encounter: Do I keep chasing new destinations, or do I return to the places that already feel like home?"

Eventually, I decided to put more than just my time into that relationship—I purchased property near the coast. Today, Nicaragua isn't simply somewhere I enjoy visiting; it's a place where I'm building toward a future.

I jokingly created my own word for what I'm working toward: The Sandbird—part snowbird, part beach bum, part traveler. The idea is simple: live between two places I love. Spend part of my time in Florida, part of my time in Nicaragua, and build a life that allows me to enjoy the best of both worlds.

Along the way, I discovered that many people share this exact dream: travelers, second-home seekers, retirees, and people looking to escape the traditional nine-to-five routine. ZIONNICA was created to share what I've learned—from finding vetted attorneys and realtors to hidden beaches, local food, and self-guided itineraries.

"And who knows? Maybe one day I'll see you somewhere in Nicaragua, sitting on a beach, enjoying a cold Toña, and wondering why we didn't do this sooner."
